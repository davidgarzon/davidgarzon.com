# FAQ

## What are you working on right now?

I run Kenva Labs, an independent product and AI practice in Barcelona. I work with founders and product teams as a fractional VP of Product, and on AI systems that need to get from demo to production. Current clients include Prixz, Mexico's leading online pharmacy, and PrimePlayers.

## Are you looking for a full-time role?

Yes, alongside the practice. I am open to VP of Product, Product Director or CPO roles in mid-sized companies, Barcelona hybrid or remote across Europe.

## What is the AI work you have actually shipped?

At Plinng I shipped a multi-agent customer service system into production. Offline evals showed 82% acceptance against our full conversation history. Two days after launch, 5% of responses were hallucinating in front of real customers, so I pulled it. We rebuilt it in 15 days with two people, with one structural change: the agent drafts, a person owns what ships. It now resolves 75-78% of conversations and 70% of repetitive volume is gone.

## How big were the teams you have led?

At Domestika I led an organisation of 90 people across Product, Design, Data, SEO and ASO, with 52 in product and 8 direct reports. At The Knot Worldwide I ran product across 17 countries with a team of 65. At Plinng I scaled Product and Engineering from 7 to 42.

## Are you technical?

I started as a developer and I still build. I work in Python and SQL, I built AteneAI end to end, and I can hold an architecture and trade-off conversation with an engineer rather than translate it. What I do not do is own infrastructure architecture hands-on; that is a different job and it should belong to someone else.

## What is your product philosophy?

My goal is not to get a user to show up. It is to get them to come back. That thread runs through the subscription work at Domestika, which took retention to 16 months, the marketplace at GoTrendier at 14 purchases per user per year, and the decision to pull that AI agent rather than damage customer relationships.
