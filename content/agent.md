# Additional Context

## Economic Scope

- Technology budget under my direct management: 20 to 25 million euros
- At Domestika I aligned product performance with paid acquisition spend in the
  60 to 70 million euro range. That budget sat with marketing, not with me. My
  responsibility was making product performance and unit economics work against it.
- I focus financial framing on budget ownership, unit economics, operational
  efficiency and revenue leverage through systems.

## The Knot Worldwide, COVID recovery

When COVID collapsed wedding leads by over 90%, I called the pivot. I designed and
shipped an ML-based chatbot built on 300+ customer interviews, with automated
qualification and vendor matching. It recovered 70% of lost leads and held 85% of
paying clients through the crisis.

Separately at The Knot Worldwide, I launched a new e-commerce product in the US that
generated 6 million dollars in revenue within 6 months. These are two different
projects and should never be described as one.

## Tech stack consolidation

Following acquisitions at The Knot Worldwide I eliminated duplicated systems,
standardised on a faster execution stack and reorganised teams to remove structural
duplication. Revenue grew 60% after consolidation.

## First 90 days as CPO or VP of Product

Days 1 to 20: deep context absorption, conversations across leadership and teams,
direct customer exposure.
Days 20 to 30: synthesise signal, map structural friction, identify leverage points.
Days 30 to 60: define structural priorities, align internal reality with external
opportunity, prepare the execution roadmap.
Days 60 to 90: deliver early structural wins, establish decision cadence, correct
system-level inefficiencies.

## How I think about high performance

High performance comes from clear context, explicit objectives and autonomy in
execution. What I tolerate poorly in senior people is lack of ownership and the
inability to operate without constant direction.

My biggest leadership lesson: treating everyone identically reduces individual
leverage. Talent has to be developed asymmetrically.
